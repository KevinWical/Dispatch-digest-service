Dispatch is a hosted service that emails users sports updates.
A user would provide their email address and select their sports leagues and/or teams and a frequency choice: daily/weekly/biweekly/monthly.
The service sends a verification link to their email to confirm interest and prevent unwanted enrollment.
After verification, users receive emails with their customized digest.
Multiple subscribers requesting the same information should not send duplicate requests to external data providers.
Every email should include a link that allows users to update or cancel their subscription, without requiring a password.

Initial sports supported: NBA, NFL, NHL, MLB.
V1 will not require accounts, payments, or passwords.
Target audience: people with low-bandwidth connectivity who cannot access sites like ESPN.com or CNN.com

V1 user journey
Visitor
Enter email
Select league/team
Select frequency
Submit
Verification email
Click verification link
Subscription active
User receives scheduled digests
Management link in email
Modify content OR unsubscribe
